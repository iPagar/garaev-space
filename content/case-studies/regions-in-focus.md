## A shared home for regional initiatives

Regions in Focus connects and supports civic initiatives working on Russian regions, both inside the country and abroad. Its website needs to explain the wider initiative while helping visitors find information relevant to a particular region.

The central challenge is the relationship between the organisation-wide story and the individual regional pages. Visitors may arrive knowing the initiative, or they may be looking for a local team and need an immediate route into the directory.

## Starting with the region

The site gives the region catalogue a clear place in the navigation and provides regional search. From the homepage, visitors can move directly to the catalogue instead of first reading every organisational page.

The directory turns a broad network into individual destinations. Regional pages and the surrounding navigation provide a structure that can expand as the initiative publishes more information.

![The regional catalogue on the Regions in Focus website](/case-studies/regions-directory.webp)

## Connecting information and participation

Alongside the regional directory, the website includes information about the initiative, areas of work and dated publications. News about programmes, research and opportunities can therefore sit within the same site as the long-lived organisational information.

Russian and English navigation supports the initiative's international context. Regional search is scoped to the selected language, keeping the search experience aligned with the content the visitor is reading.

## The result

The public site provides an entry point into a distributed initiative: visitors can learn about its purpose, browse regions, read updates and use the published contact information. The project demonstrates how a content-led website can support discovery across a network of teams.
