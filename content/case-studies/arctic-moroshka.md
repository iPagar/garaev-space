## A website built around the award cycle

Arctic Moroshka is an independent journalism award covering the Russian Arctic and the North. Its website has to serve several audiences: journalists considering an application, readers exploring the nominated work and people looking for the jury, rules or past results.

The development task was to give these activities a shared structure that can accommodate successive award cycles. The award continues beyond a single announcement or ceremony, so the current programme and its archive need to remain understandable together.

## Making the current programme visible

The homepage introduces the award, surfaces the current stage and provides an application entry point. Dates and cycle information give visitors context before they decide where to go next. Supporting pages explain the award and introduce the people involved.

Nominees and results are organised by cycle. That gives each edition a stable destination and avoids mixing the current selection with previous winners.

![Arctic Moroshka nominee catalogue organised around an award cycle](/case-studies/moroshka-nominees.webp)

## Content that can follow the programme

The site uses Next.js and Sanity, with content structures for cycles, nominees, results and the award team. The CMS supports changes to programme content while the website provides consistent presentation and navigation.

Russian and English routes make language part of the content structure. Application and contact forms use explicit validation, so the website can identify missing or invalid information before processing a submission.

## The result

The published website brings the award introduction, application entry point, nominee catalogue and results archive into one place. A visitor can understand the programme, explore a specific cycle and find a way to participate or contact the organisers.
