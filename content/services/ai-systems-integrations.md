## AI that fits into your team’s work

I design and develop AI features around the information, tools and decisions your team already works with. The starting point is a specific task: finding material, extracting useful data, preparing a research result or helping someone navigate a complex workflow.

An engagement can cover a focused integration in an existing product or a dedicated application with its own interface and backend.

## What you can bring

- Researchers spend time moving information between sources and manually assembling a result.
- Your team needs a way to ask questions of its documents or use external tools from one workspace.
- An AI prototype needs application logic, access controls and clear handling of incomplete results.
- A product needs an AI feature connected to its existing accounts, data and workflows.

## What I can build

The scope can include a conversational or task-based interface, connections to your data and external APIs, document processing and structured outputs. Where work takes time, background jobs and visible progress keep the interaction understandable.

Tool integration includes defining what the model can request, how the application executes that request and how the result returns to the user. Source material, generated explanations and processing states need clear roles in the interface.

## How the work is scoped

We begin with examples of the task and the result your team considers useful. I map the available sources, access requirements and points where a person needs to review the output. That gives us a concrete workflow to implement and assess.

Evaluation uses representative inputs, including incomplete or unexpected responses. Model choice, running costs and the handling of sensitive information are decisions to make in the context of that workflow.

## What you receive

An implemented feature or application, the integrations needed for the agreed task, and documentation covering configuration and operation. Deployment, usage monitoring and further iterations can be included in the engagement.

To start, describe the task, the sources involved and the part of the current process that takes the most effort.
